About

Training models, then shipping them.

I’m a B.Tech AI & ML student who likes the whole path: preparing data, validating a model, and putting it somewhere people can use it. I trained and deployed a medical image segmentation model to HuggingFace Spaces, built local-first computer vision and RAG tools, and delivered a full-stack academic management system in a month as an AI Intern.

I work in PyTorch, React and Python, and I care about keeping data private, so most of what I build runs locally.

Polyp Segmentation
